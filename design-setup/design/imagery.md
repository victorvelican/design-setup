# Imagery and film

## The world
One world for every image and film: a brass laboratory balance, cream ledger paper with ink tally marks, a black fountain pen, low morning light, dust in the light, one small terracotta object. 35 mm look, grain, natural motion blur, no camera shake. Patient, exact, unhurried.

## What generated media may never show
Text, numbers, letters, interface, screens, devices, logos, people, faces. A hand may hold the pen and nothing else. Every still is inspected at full size before it is used; anything with a letter-like mark, a numeral on an instrument, a second instrument, a finger at the frame edge, is rejected and re-rolled.

## Placement
Up to two plates per page; a film only where a page can carry it, with a poster fallback and reduced-motion off. Full-bleed bands on a desk, rounded frames on a phone. The page must read identically with every asset removed; the assets are atmosphere, never information.

## Budgets
A page arrives under 300 KiB on a real connection, measured, with the stated exceptions (a film page, the page that contains the tool). The generating tool is never named in HTML, alt text or file names. Credits spent are recorded per asset.
